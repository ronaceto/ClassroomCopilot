AI for Explaining Difficult Concepts 
One of the best uses of AI in science is asking it to explain ideas more clearly. A student 
who does not fully understand osmosis, Newton’s laws, chemical bonding, or the rock 
cycle may benefit from a simpler explanation or an analogy. A biology student, for 
example, might ask for photosynthesis to be explained in simple terms with an everyday 
comparison, while another student might ask for the difference between two related 
concepts to be broken down in a more memorable way. 
Explain photosynthesis in simple terms for a high school biology student and 
include an everyday analogy. 
That kind of prompt can make the concept easier to understand without lowering the 
level of thinking. Students can also ask for step-by-step explanations or for the 
difference between two related concepts. 
These uses are especially helpful in science because many topics build on earlier 
understanding. If one idea is unclear, later ideas often become harder too. 
Using AI for Scientific Vocabulary and Comparison 
Science includes a large amount of specialized vocabulary. Students may know the 
general idea of a lesson but still get stuck on terms such as velocity, equilibrium, 
ecosystem, or catalyst. 
AI can help students define terms, compare related ideas, and explain how concepts 
connect. A student might ask: 
What is the difference between speed and velocity? 
Or: 
Compare mitosis and meiosis in a way that is easy to remember. 
These prompts are useful because science often depends on distinguishing ideas that 
sound similar but are not the same.

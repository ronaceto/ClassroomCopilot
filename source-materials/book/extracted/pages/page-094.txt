Student Example 
A student named Elena is reviewing cellular respiration for a biology test. Her notes feel 
complicated, and she keeps mixing up the role of mitochondria and glucose. 
She asks AI: 
Explain cellular respiration in simple terms for a high school biology student and 
compare it to how a car uses fuel. 
Then she asks: 
Now give me five review questions on cellular respiration and explain any I miss. 
AI helped Elena understand the concept and practice it, but she still used her textbook 
and class notes to confirm the details before the test. 
Classroom Idea 
Teachers can ask students to compare an AI explanation of a science concept with the 
explanation in a textbook or class notes. Students can identify: 
 what the AI explained clearly 
 what the AI left out 
 whether the analogy helped or oversimplified the idea 
 what still needs verification 
This helps students learn that scientific explanation must be accurate as well as 
understandable. 
Try This 
Use the prompt below to see whether a second explanation helps the concept click 
more clearly. Then compare the response to your class notes or textbook so the 
easier explanation still stays tied to accurate science. 
Prompt Example 
Explain this science concept in simple terms, compare it to something from everyday 
life, and then ask me three review questions to check my understanding. 
After using the prompt, ask yourself: 
 Did the explanation match what my teacher or textbook says? 
 What part still feels uncertain? 
 What evidence or class material would help confirm it?

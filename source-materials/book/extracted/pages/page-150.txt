AI Research Tools 
Research tools are different from chat assistants because they are more focused on 
helping users locate, organize, and verify information. 
These tools can be especially useful when students are: 
 starting a research paper 
 exploring a historical topic 
 gathering source-based information 
 comparing multiple sources 
 checking claims or facts 
Examples include: 
 Perplexity 
 Elicit 
 NotebookLM 
These tools are often more useful when students need information connected to sources 
rather than a simple conversational explanation. 
Perplexity 
Perplexity is especially helpful for research because it often combines AI-generated 
responses with source links. This makes it easier for students to move from an 
explanation to actual supporting material. 
Students can use Perplexity to: 
 research current or historical topics 
 gather source-based overviews 
 identify useful articles or references 
 compare different explanations 
 verify claims more easily 
This makes it a particularly strong tool for projects, essays, and presentations where 
source awareness matters.

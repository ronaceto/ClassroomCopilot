Suggest two ways to make this paragraph more clear without changing my 
meaning. 
Or: 
What is one place in this essay where I need stronger evidence? 
These are strong uses because the student’s own draft remains at the center. The AI is 
helping the writer improve, not replacing the writer. 
This is very different from asking AI to write the introduction, body paragraphs, or 
conclusion. If the tool is doing the composing, then the student is missing the chance to 
build one of the core skills ELA is designed to teach. 
Protecting Voice and Interpretation 
In ELA, student voice matters. 
A good paragraph is not just grammatically correct. It also reflects the student’s 
interpretation, tone, and choices as a writer. If AI rewrites too much, the work may 
sound polished but generic. It may lose what makes the writing feel personal and 
authentic. 
This matters especially in literary analysis. A novel, poem, or speech can often be 
interpreted in more than one way. Students need practice developing their own 
interpretation and supporting it with evidence. AI may suggest possibilities, but the 
student must still decide what they think and why. 
A useful rule is this: Use AI to improve your writing, not to replace your voice. 
Responsible and Irresponsible Uses in ELA 
Responsible uses of AI in ELA include: 
 clarifying difficult passages 
 brainstorming themes, claims, or essay angles 
 generating vocabulary practice or discussion questions 
 getting feedback on clarity, structure, or transitions 
 preparing for class discussion or oral presentation 
Irresponsible uses include: 
 asking AI to write a full essay or paragraph that will be submitted as student 
work 
 using AI summaries instead of reading the assigned text

The Prompt Pyramid 
A helpful way to think about prompting is to imagine a pyramid. At the bottom are very 
simple prompts. As you move upward, prompts become more powerful because they 
include more structure and more useful detail. 
Basic prompt: “Explain the water cycle.” Better prompt: “Explain the water cycle step 
by step.” Strong prompt: “Explain the water cycle step by step for a middle school 
student and include a simple real-world example.”

The Four Parts of a Strong Prompt 
Strong prompts often include four parts: context, task, audience, and format. Context 
tells the AI what situation matters. The task explains what you want it to do. The 
audience tells the AI who the explanation is for. The format tells the AI how to organize 
the answer.

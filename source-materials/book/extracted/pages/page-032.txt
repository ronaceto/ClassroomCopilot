Why This Chapter Matters 
Research is one of the clearest places where students can benefit from AI while also 
seeing its limits. 
AI can save time, generate ideas, and help students organize thinking. But it cannot 
replace judgment, source evaluation, or careful reading. Students still need to decide 
what is accurate, what is missing, and what evidence supports a claim. 
That is why AI works best as a research assistant—not as a research substitute. 
Chapter Summary 
In this chapter, you learned how AI can help students begin research, generate better 
questions, explore multiple perspectives, and organize understanding. You also saw why 
verification is essential and why critical thinking remains at the center of strong research.

As you continue using AI, challenge yourself to use it in ways that make you more 
thoughtful, not less. 
Try to be the kind of student who: 
 asks better questions 
 looks for understanding, not just speed 
 checks information before trusting it 
 uses technology to support effort, not escape it 
 keeps curiosity at the center of learning 
Those habits will matter long after this book is finished.

What is one skill I can start building now that will still matter in an AI-driven future? 
Give me three practical ways a high school student could start developing that skill this 
year. 
 Which skill feels most important to me? 
 What am I already doing that helps build it? 
 What is one next step I can take? 
This is a good reminder that AI is most powerful when it helps students take action on 
their own growth.

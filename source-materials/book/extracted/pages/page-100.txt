Try This 
Use the prompt below to turn a general wellness goal into something more realistic 
and specific. As you read, think about which suggestions feel practical for your life 
and which ones would still need support from a trusted adult. 
Prompt Example 
Help me create a simple, healthy weekly routine for sleep, hydration, movement, and 
school focus. Then give me three reflection questions I can use to see what is 
working. 
After reading the response, ask yourself: 
 Which parts feel realistic for my life right now? 
 What should I discuss with a teacher, coach, counselor, or parent instead of 
asking AI? 
 How can I protect my privacy while still learning from the tool? 
Chapter Summary 
In this chapter, you learned that AI can support PE, health, and personal wellness by 
clarifying concepts, helping students reflect, and organizing goals or routines. You also 
learned that health and wellness topics require strong privacy habits and human support. 
AI can be a useful planning and learning tool, but it should never replace trusted adults, 
teachers, coaches, counselors, or medical professionals.

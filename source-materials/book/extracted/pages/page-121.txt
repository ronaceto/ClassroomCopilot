Classroom Idea 
Teachers can use this chapter as part of a discussion on future readiness. 
Students might work in groups to choose a career field—such as healthcare, education, 
engineering, media, or law—and investigate how AI may affect that field. They can then 
present: 
 one way AI could help people in that job 
 one risk or challenge AI may create 
 one human skill that will still remain important 
This kind of activity helps students see that AI is not just a school topic. It is part of the 
future they are entering. 
Try This 
Open an AI tool and ask: 
Prompt Example 
What careers combine technology, creativity, and problem solving? 
 
Then follow up with: 
Prompt Example 
Choose one of those careers and explain what skills a high school student should start 
building now. 
 
As you read the response, ask yourself: 
 Which skills seem most important? 
 Which of those skills am I already building in school? 
 What would I need to keep practicing if I wanted to prepare for that kind of 
work? 
This activity helps turn AI into a tool for self-reflection, not just information gathering.

Chapter 20 — The 25 Powerful Prompts Every 
Student Should Know 
 
Key Question 
What kinds of prompts help students get the most useful, responsible, and 
educational results from AI? 
Learning Objectives 
 Recognize what makes a prompt useful for students 
 Learn prompt patterns that support studying, writing, research, and creativity 
 Use prompts to deepen learning rather than avoid it 
 Build a practical toolkit of questions that can be reused across subjects 
Introduction 
The quality of AI responses depends heavily on the quality of the prompts 
students use. A vague prompt often produces a vague answer, while a thoughtful 
prompt can lead to a clearer explanation, better examples, stronger feedback, and more 
useful support. This matters because AI is not just a tool for getting information. It is a 
tool for asking better questions. 
Students who learn how to write strong prompts gain a real advantage. They can study 
more effectively, organize ideas more clearly, explore topics more deeply, and use AI in 
ways that support learning rather than replace it. This chapter gathers some of the most 
useful prompts in the entire book and organizes them into practical categories, not as 
rigid scripts to memorize, but as a toolkit students can adapt to many situations. 
The best way to use this chapter is not to memorize every prompt exactly. Instead, pay 
attention to the structure of the prompts and the kinds of support they create. Over 
time, students should learn to shape these prompts to fit their own classes, projects, and 
learning goals, just as they would adapt any other academic strategy. 
What Makes a Prompt Powerful? 
A powerful prompt usually does at least one of the following: 
 gives the AI a clear task 
 explains the intended audience or level 
 asks for a useful format 
 encourages explanation instead of just answers 
 creates opportunities for practice or feedback

Figure 18.1 — Verifying AI-Generated Media 
This figure illustrates a verification path that begins with a suspicious post, image, or clip 
and moves through a pause, source check, reverse-image search or cross-check, evidence 
review, and a final decision about whether the content deserves trust. 
Students do not just need to learn how to use AI. They also need to learn how to live in 
a world where AI changes what information looks like. That means media literacy is no 
longer optional. It is part of responsible digital citizenship, academic judgment, and 
everyday life. 
The students who navigate this world best will not be the ones who trust everything or 
reject everything. They will be the ones who know how to pause, verify, and decide what 
is worth believing. 
Chapter Summary 
In this chapter, you learned that AI can be used to generate misinformation, 
manipulated media, false summaries, and believable content that does not deserve trust. 
You also learned that strong media literacy depends on verification, healthy skepticism, 
and evidence-based judgment. In an AI-rich world, students need more than fast 
reactions. They need strong habits for deciding what is real before they trust or share it. 
In the next chapter, we will look at how AI is changing careers, industries, and the future 
of work—and why students who understand AI literacy will be better prepared for what 
comes next.

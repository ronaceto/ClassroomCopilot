Chatbot 
A computer program designed to simulate conversation with a user through text or 
voice. Many modern AI chatbots are powered by large language models. 
Collaboration 
Working together to complete a task or solve a problem. In the context of this book, 
students can think of AI as a collaborative tool, but not a replacement for human effort 
or teamwork. 
Concept Map 
A visual diagram that shows how ideas connect to one another. Concept maps help 
students organize information and see relationships between topics. 
Context 
The background information included in a prompt or task that helps AI produce a more 
useful response. Context makes prompts clearer and more specific. 
Critical Evaluation 
The process of carefully examining information before accepting it as accurate or useful. 
This is especially important when using AI-generated content. 
Critical Thinking 
The habit of analyzing ideas carefully, asking good questions, checking evidence, and 
making thoughtful judgments instead of accepting information too quickly. 
Data 
Information used by computers to process tasks, recognize patterns, and make 
predictions. AI systems rely on large amounts of data during training and use. 
Data Set 
A collection of related data used for training, testing, or analyzing an AI system. 
Debugging 
The process of finding and fixing errors in computer code. AI can help students debug 
code, but students still need to understand the logic behind the fix.

For example, students can ask AI to:

 create a short review sheet

A student preparing for a science test might ask: 
Turn these notes into a study guide with key terms and definitions. 
That response can then be used as a review sheet, a self-test, or the basis for 
practice questions. 
The important thing is that the student is still responsible for checking the 
information and understanding the material. AI can help organize learning, but it 
should not replace the work of learning. 
Student Example 
A student has a quiz tomorrow on the causes of the French Revolution. They feel 
overwhelmed by the amount of information in their notes. 
They ask AI: 
Summarize the three main causes of the French Revolution in simple terms for a 
high school student. 
Then they ask: 
Create five quiz questions based on that summary. 
After answering the questions, they follow up with: 
Explain the answers I missed. 
In less than fifteen minutes, the student has reviewed key ideas, tested their 
understanding, and learned from mistakes. That is much stronger studying than simply 
rereading the chapter.

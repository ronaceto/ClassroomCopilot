Why This Chapter Matters 
Problem solving is one of the clearest places where AI can either support learning or 
weaken it. 
If students use AI only for final answers, they may become more dependent and less 
confident over time. But if they use AI to understand processes, compare examples, and 
practice independently, they can become much stronger problem solvers. 
That is why this chapter matters. It teaches students how to keep control of the learning 
process while still benefiting from AI’s speed and flexibility. 
Chapter Summary 
In this chapter, you learned how AI can support problem solving by explaining steps, 
highlighting patterns, and generating practice opportunities. You also explored why 
strong prompts improve problem-solving support and why copying answers weakens 
learning. 
In the next chapter, we will look at how AI can help students with one of the most

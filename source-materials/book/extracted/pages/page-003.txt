About This Book 
Artificial intelligence is rapidly changing how people learn, work, communicate, and 
create. This book helps students understand how AI works and how to use it as a tool 
for learning rather than a shortcut that replaces thinking. It also gives teachers, families, 
and schools practical language and guidance for shaping ethical, age-appropriate, school-
aligned AI use. 
Who This Book Is For 
 Students in grades 8-12 who want to use AI wisely for school, learning, creativity, 
and future readiness. 
 Teachers who need a practical AI literacy curriculum without turning the class into a 
coding camp. 
 Parents and caregivers who want students to use AI safely, honestly, and 
productively at home. 
 School leaders and counselors looking for language, routines, and projects that 
support responsible AI use. 
Tool access, age requirements, privacy settings, and district approval vary. Always follow 
school, family, and platform rules when using AI tools. 
How to Use This Book 
 Read the student chapters in order for a complete AI literacy foundation. 
 Use the “Try This” prompts to practice responsible AI use. 
 Use the cross-subject chapters to apply AI in ELA, math, history, science, health, 
and project work. 
 Use Appendix A and the end resources to extend project work, reflection, and 
responsible AI use. 
 Use the glossary, recommended tools, and recommended resources as reference 
sections throughout the book.

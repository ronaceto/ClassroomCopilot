Chapter 8 — AI for Problem Solving 
 
Key Question 
How can students use AI to solve problems more effectively without depending on it 
for every answer? 
Learning Objectives 
 Understand how AI can support step-by-step problem solving 
 Learn how AI can help students recognize patterns in math and science 
 Use AI to clarify confusing steps and strengthen reasoning 
 Practice using AI as a guide rather than a shortcut 
Introduction 
Many students find problem solving frustrating. 
This is especially true in subjects like math, physics, chemistry, and economics, where 
one wrong step can make an entire answer fall apart. Even when students understand 
the topic in general, they may still struggle to figure out what to do first, how to move 
from one step to the next, or why a solution works the way it does. 
This is one area where AI can be genuinely helpful. 
Artificial intelligence can break down complicated problems, explain processes step by 
step, and offer examples that help students see patterns more clearly. It can also respond 
to follow-up questions in a way that feels more flexible than a worksheet or textbook. 
But there is a risk here too. 
If students use AI only to get the final answer, they may finish faster, but they often 
learn less. The real value of AI in problem solving is not the answer itself. It is the 
support it gives students while they learn the process. 
This chapter is about using AI to strengthen reasoning, not replace it.

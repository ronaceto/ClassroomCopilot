This is also where AI can make learning feel more interactive. It can help students 
explore ideas that might otherwise feel abstract or one-sided. 
The AI Time Machine 
One especially engaging research strategy is to use AI to simulate historical or 
perspective-based interviews. 
This approach allows students to ask questions as though they were speaking directly to 
a person from the time period they are studying. While the response is still generated by 
AI and must be evaluated carefully, it can help students think more actively about 
motives, choices, and historical context. 
For example, a student might ask: 
Act as George Washington in 1777 during the American Revolution. Answer my 
questions as if you are speaking to a student about the war. 
Then follow up with questions such as: 
 What challenges are you facing as a leader? 
 Why did the colonies choose to fight Britain? 
 What are you most worried about? 
This type of simulated interview helps students imagine historical events as lived 
experiences rather than distant facts. It encourages curiosity, perspective-taking, and 
better questioning. 
It is important, however, to remember that the AI response is still an interpretation. 
Students should use this strategy as a way to explore ideas, not as a substitute for real 
historical sources. 
Why Verification Matters 
This is where AI research must be handled carefully. 
AI can generate a smooth and convincing answer even when part of the information is 
inaccurate, incomplete, or oversimplified. A student who relies entirely on AI without 
checking other sources may end up repeating errors without realizing it. 
That is why verification is essential.

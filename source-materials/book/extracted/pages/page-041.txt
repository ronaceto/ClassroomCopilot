AI as a Brainstorming Partner 
One of the hardest parts of writing is simply getting started. 
Students may know the general topic of an assignment but still feel uncertain about what 
angle to take, what claim to make, or what ideas are worth developing. In those 
moments, AI can act like a brainstorming partner. 
For example, a student writing about the impact of social media might ask: 
Help me brainstorm three possible arguments about how social media affects teenagers. 
That kind of prompt does not ask AI to write the essay. It asks AI to generate starting 
points. 
This is an important distinction. 
Used well, AI can help students: 
 generate possible essay angles 
 identify pros and cons of an issue 
 suggest examples that support a topic 
 turn a broad idea into a narrower focus 
 overcome the fear of a blank page 
The goal of brainstorming is not to let AI decide what the student thinks. It is to 
generate possibilities so the student can choose, shape, and develop ideas more 
confidently. In that sense, AI works best as a starting-point generator rather than as a 
substitute for real writing decisions. 
Turning Ideas Into Outlines 
Once students have a direction, the next challenge is organization. 
A good piece of writing needs structure. It should have a clear beginning, logical 
development, and a strong ending. Many students understand their topic but struggle 
with how to arrange their ideas into a coherent outline. 
AI can help with this stage too. 
A student might ask: 
Create an outline for a persuasive essay about renewable energy for a high school 
student.

Innovation 
The process of creating or improving ideas, systems, or tools in ways that add value or 
solve problems. 
Iteration 
The process of improving something through repeated attempts. In AI use, students 
often improve results by refining prompts over time. 
Large Language Model (LLM) 
A type of AI trained on large amounts of text so it can recognize patterns in language 
and generate human-like responses. Many AI chat assistants are powered by large 
language models. 
Machine Learning 
A field of AI in which computers learn patterns from data rather than being explicitly 
programmed for every single task. 
Model 
A trained AI system that has learned patterns from data and can use those patterns to 
generate outputs such as predictions, text, or classifications. 
Natural Language Processing (NLP) 
A field of AI focused on helping computers understand, interpret, and generate human 
language. 
Neural Network 
A computing system inspired loosely by the structure of the human brain. Neural 
networks help AI systems detect patterns and make predictions. 
Output 
The response or result produced by a system after it processes input. 
Pattern Recognition 
The ability to identify repeated relationships or structures within data. This is a core part 
of how many AI systems work.

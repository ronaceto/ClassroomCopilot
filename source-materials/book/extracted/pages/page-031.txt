Classroom Idea 
Teachers can use AI as part of a research literacy lesson by asking students to compare 
an AI-generated explanation with textbook or source-based material. 
For example, students might ask AI to summarize the Civil Rights Movement, then 
compare that answer with a class handout or textbook chapter. They can look for: 
 accurate information 
 missing details 
 oversimplified explanations 
 possible errors 
This kind of activity teaches students that research is not just about getting information. 
It is about evaluating information. 
Try This 
Use the two prompts below to move from a broad topic toward more focused 
inquiry. As you read the responses, pay attention to what seems useful, what still 
needs verification, and which question would lead to the strongest project. 
Prompt Example 
Give me an overview of the Industrial Revolution and explain why it changed society. 
 
Prompt Example 
Suggest three research questions a high school student could explore about the 
Industrial Revolution.

Why This Chapter Matters 
Students do not need AI to become better at finishing tasks quickly. In many cases, they 
can already find information online or copy answers from somewhere else. 
What makes AI different is that it can support a more interactive kind of learning. 
When students use AI well, they are not just getting information. They are engaging in a 
conversation that can clarify confusion, generate practice, and support reflection. This is 
why AI should be treated as a learning partner rather than an answer machine. 
Chapter Summary 
In this chapter, you learned that AI is most useful when it helps students build 
understanding rather than simply collect answers. You explored how follow-up 
questions deepen learning, how AI can support different learning styles, and why 
explanation plus practice is more powerful than explanation alone. 
In the next chapter, we will return to one of the most important practical skills in the

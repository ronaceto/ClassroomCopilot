Using AI to Start Research 
One of the best uses of AI in research is at the beginning of the process, when students 
need help understanding the big picture. For example, a student beginning a project on 
the Industrial Revolution might ask for an overview of the topic and why it changed 
society. A prompt like that can help the student identify major themes before moving 
into more detailed sources. AI is especially useful for generating background knowledge, 
surfacing key terms, and helping students see possible angles for a project or essay. 
Generating Better Research Questions 
Strong research depends on strong questions. 
Many students begin with topics that are too broad. For example, “World War II” is not 
really a research question. It is a huge topic. A better research process begins when 
students narrow their focus and ask something more specific. 
AI can help with that. 
A student might ask: 
Help me narrow my topic on climate change into a question suitable for a school 
presentation. 
This is one of AI’s most useful roles in research. It can help students move from a broad 
topic toward a clearer, more manageable question. That shift improves the quality of the 
entire project. 
Exploring Perspectives 
Good research often involves looking at more than one point of view. 
This is especially important in subjects such as history, government, literature, and social 
studies, where events and ideas are often interpreted differently depending on 
perspective. AI can help students compare viewpoints and see how different groups or 
individuals may have understood the same event. 
For example, a student might ask: 
Explain the American Revolution from both the British and colonial perspectives. 
Or: 
Compare how different groups were affected by the Industrial Revolution. 
These kinds of prompts help students move beyond memorizing facts and begin 
thinking more deeply about interpretation, cause and effect, and historical context.

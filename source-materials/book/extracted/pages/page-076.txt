Chapter 12 — AI in English Language Arts 
 
Key Question 
How can students use AI to become stronger readers, writers, speakers, and thinkers in 
English language arts without losing their own voice or doing dishonest work? 
Learning Objectives 
 Understand how AI can support reading, writing, vocabulary, and discussion 
preparation in ELA. 
 Recognize the difference between using AI as a writing coach and using AI to 
replace student work. 
 Practice prompts that help students brainstorm, revise, and reflect more effectively. 
 Use verification, teacher expectations, and human judgment when working with AI-
generated suggestions. 
 Protect student voice, interpretation, and originality while using AI tools. 
Introduction 
English language arts is about much more than finishing assignments. It is where 
students learn to read closely, interpret meaning, communicate clearly, develop 
arguments, and express ideas in their own voice. Those skills matter in every subject, but 
they are at the center of ELA. 
Because AI tools can generate writing quickly, students may assume ELA is one of the 
easiest places to misuse them. In reality, ELA is one of the best places to learn how to 
use AI well. AI can help students brainstorm, clarify ideas, revise sentences, prepare for 
discussions, and deepen understanding of texts. But it should not replace the student’s 
reading, interpretation, or writing process. 
This chapter explores how AI can support ELA learning in ways that strengthen 
thinking rather than weaken it. The goal is not to let AI become the writer. The goal is 
to help students become better readers, writers, and communicators. 
Picture a student walking into English class worried about a discussion on a novel they 
did not fully understand the night before. Instead of staying lost, the student uses AI 
beforehand to unpack a difficult passage, surface two possible themes, and rehearse a 
few discussion ideas. That kind of support can make class participation more confident 
without replacing the student’s reading or interpretation.

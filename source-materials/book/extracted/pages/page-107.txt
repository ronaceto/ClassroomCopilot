Using AI to Strengthen Learning 
The most responsible use of AI is the kind that clearly strengthens learning. 
Students are usually on solid ground when they use AI to: 
 ask for clearer explanations 
 generate practice questions 
 check understanding 
 compare perspectives 
 organize notes 
 improve the clarity of their own writing 
 break down a large task into smaller steps 
These uses support learning while keeping the student actively involved. 
Students move into less responsible territory when they use AI to: 
 write graded work for them 
 complete problem sets without understanding 
 produce answers they cannot explain 
 bypass effort rather than support it 
This distinction should guide every decision. 
Student Example 
A student named Daniel is assigned a short history response about the causes of the 
Civil War. 
He is not sure how to begin, so he asks AI: 
Help me understand the main causes of the Civil War in simple terms. 
After reading the explanation, Daniel checks his class notes and textbook. Then he asks: 
Help me organize these ideas into a short outline for a paragraph response. 
Using that outline, Daniel writes the paragraph himself. 
This is a responsible use of AI. The tool helped him understand the topic and organize 
his thinking, but the final work still reflected his own understanding. 
Now imagine a different version. Daniel asks AI to write the full paragraph and turns it 
in without revising or understanding it. That is a very different situation. 
The tool may be the same, but the use is not.

Privacy and Personal Boundaries 
Students must be especially careful about privacy in this subject area. 
They should avoid entering sensitive personal information into AI tools, including: 
 private health details 
 mental health crises or emergency concerns 
 other students’ personal situations 
 medical records or school health information 
If a concern is serious, personal, or urgent, the right response is to talk to a trusted adult, 
counselor, school nurse, coach, parent, or medical professional—not to depend on AI. 
What AI Should Not Replace 
AI should never replace professional or teacher guidance in health and wellness matters. 
Students should not use AI to: 
 diagnose injuries or illnesses 
 replace school-approved health instruction 
 follow unverified training or nutrition advice 
 handle personal emergencies 
 make major decisions without adult support 
AI can help with general learning and planning, but real well-being depends on human 
support, safe judgment, and trusted guidance. 
Responsible and Irresponsible Uses in PE, Health, and 
Wellness 
Responsible uses include: 
 clarifying class concepts in PE or health 
 organizing goals and healthy routines 
 creating reflection questions for journals or projects 
 reviewing terms related to fitness, nutrition, or sleep 
 planning for school-based wellness activities in general, non-sensitive ways

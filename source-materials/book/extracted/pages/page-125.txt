In other words, strong prompts guide the AI toward being helpful in a specific way. 
For example, compare these two prompts: 
Weak prompt: 
Explain photosynthesis. 
Stronger prompt: 
Explain photosynthesis in simple terms for a high school student, then give me three 
quiz questions to check my understanding. 
The second version works better because it asks for explanation and practice. That is 
what students should look for throughout this chapter. 
Prompts for Learning and Understanding 
These prompts are useful when students are trying to understand a concept more clearly. 
1. Explain a concept simply 
Explain this concept in simple terms for a high school student. 
Why it works: 
This prompt helps students move from confusion to clarity by asking the AI to simplify 
the explanation without making it childish. 
2. Teach step by step 
Teach me this topic step by step as if you were my tutor. 
Why it works: 
This encourages a guided explanation rather than a quick summary. 
3. Use an analogy 
Explain this concept using a real-world analogy. 
Why it works: 
Analogies help students connect unfamiliar ideas to familiar experiences.

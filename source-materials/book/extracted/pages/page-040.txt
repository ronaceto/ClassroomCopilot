Chapter 7 — AI for Writing, Revision, and 
Communication 
 
Key Question 
How can students use AI to support writing, revision, and communication without 
losing their own voice and ideas? 
Learning Objectives 
 Understand how AI can support prewriting, drafting, and revision. 
 Learn how AI can help organize ideas, strengthen clarity, and adjust writing for 
audience and purpose. 
 Recognize the difference between using AI for feedback and using AI to replace 
student work. 
 Protect student voice while improving communication across school 
assignments and presentations. 
Introduction 
Writing can be one of the most challenging parts of school. 
Students are often asked to turn ideas into essays, reports, stories, reflections, and 
presentations. Even when they understand the topic, many still struggle with how to 
begin. They may stare at a blank page, unsure of how to organize their thoughts, how to 
build a strong argument, or how to make their writing more clear and engaging. 
This is one area where artificial intelligence can be especially helpful when students are 
shaping ideas into clear, effective writing. 
AI can support writing by helping students brainstorm starting points, organize 
information, revise for clarity, and improve how ideas are communicated to a reader or 
audience. 
However, writing is also an area where students must be especially careful. If AI begins 
doing too much of the thinking, planning, or drafting, then students lose something 
important: the chance to develop their own voice. 
This chapter is about using AI to support writing, revision, and communication in ways 
that strengthen student thinking rather than replace it.

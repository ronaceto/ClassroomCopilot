Why AI Literacy Matters 
AI literacy means understanding what AI is, how it works at a basic level, what it can do 
well, where it makes mistakes, and how to use it responsibly. Students who build AI 
literacy are better prepared not only for school, but also for future careers and a world 
where intelligent tools will be increasingly common.

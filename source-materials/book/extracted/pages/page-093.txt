AI and Scientific Questions 
AI can also help students ask better science questions. 
Science learning becomes stronger when students are curious about how and why things 
happen. AI can support that curiosity by generating follow-up questions, possible 
hypotheses, or review prompts. 
For example, a student studying ecosystems might ask: 
Give me three questions a scientist might ask about how pollution affects a 
freshwater ecosystem. 
This is useful because it helps students think like investigators rather than only 
memorizing facts. 
What AI Cannot Replace in Science 
Science is not only about explanation. It is also about evidence. 
That means AI should not replace: 
 teacher-approved lab procedures 
 actual observations from experiments 
 data collection and interpretation 
 safety directions in class or labs 
 source-checked scientific claims 
Students should be especially careful not to use AI for safety advice, medical decisions, 
or unapproved lab instructions. AI can help students understand lab ideas, but it should 
never replace teacher supervision or proper scientific procedure. 
Using AI Responsibly in Science 
In science, responsible use means letting AI support explanation and review while still 
relying on evidence, observation, and approved procedures. AI can help students 
understand concepts and ask better questions, but it should not stand in for real data, 
safe lab directions, or conclusions that need evidence. Science remains strongest when 
students use AI to learn the concepts while trusting experiments, sources, and teacher 
guidance for what counts as valid scientific work. 
A useful rule in science is this: Let AI help you understand the science, but let 
evidence and proper procedure guide what you conclude.

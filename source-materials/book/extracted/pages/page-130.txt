Prompts for Creativity and Projects 
These prompts help students generate new ideas and push projects in more 
original directions. 
25. Expand a project idea 
Give me three creative ways to make this project more original or interesting. 
Why it works: 
This encourages students to move beyond the first obvious idea and think more 
imaginatively. 
How to Adapt These Prompts 
Students should not think of these 25 prompts as fixed scripts that must always be used 
exactly as written. 
Instead, they should think of them as patterns. 
A strong prompt can usually be improved by adding: 
 the subject or topic 
 the grade level 
 the format wanted 
 the purpose of the response 
For example, a student could adapt a general quiz prompt into: 
Create five quiz questions about the causes of the French Revolution for a high school 
history student. 
Or a writing prompt into: 
Suggest two ways I can improve the clarity of this science lab conclusion without 
changing my main meaning. 
The more clearly students define what they need, the more useful the response usually 
becomes.

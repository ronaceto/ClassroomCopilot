Turn this topic into a simple set of flashcards with questions on one side and answers on

Prompts for Research and Critical Thinking 
These prompts help students begin exploring a topic and thinking more deeply about it. 
11. Start a topic overview 
Give me a clear overview of this topic and explain why it matters. 
Why it works: 
This is useful at the beginning of research when a student needs background knowledge. 
12. Generate research questions 
Suggest three research questions a high school student could explore about this topic. 
Why it works: 
This helps students move from a broad topic to a more focused direction. 
13. Compare perspectives 
Explain this issue from two different perspectives. 
Why it works: 
This helps students see complexity and avoid oversimplified thinking. 
14. Build a timeline 
Create a simple timeline of the key events related to this topic. 
Why it works: 
Timelines help students organize historical or sequential information clearly.

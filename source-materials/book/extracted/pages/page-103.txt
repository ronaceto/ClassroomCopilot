Responsibility Begins With Purpose 
The first question students should ask when using AI is simple: 
Why am I using this tool? 
That question matters because the same AI feature can be used responsibly in one 
situation and irresponsibly in another. 
For example, asking AI to explain a difficult math concept can be a strong use of the 
tool. Asking AI to complete a graded assignment that is supposed to measure your own 
understanding is something very different. 
Purpose changes everything. 
Students who use AI responsibly are usually trying to do one or more of the following: 
 understand a concept more clearly 
 generate practice questions 
 organize ideas 
 improve a draft 
 review for a test 
 explore different perspectives 
 get help with the next step in a process 
In each of those cases, the student is still doing the learning. 
By contrast, students who use AI irresponsibly often use it to avoid effort rather than 
support growth. They may ask for finished essays, completed homework answers, or 
work they could not explain themselves. 
The difference is not whether AI is involved. The difference is whether the student is 
still doing the thinking.

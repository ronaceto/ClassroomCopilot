Robotics 
A field of technology focused on machines that can perform physical tasks, often with 
some level of automation or AI support. 
Simulation 
A representation of a real-world process or scenario. AI can be used in simulations for 
training, learning, and experimentation. 
Source 
The place where information comes from, such as a book, website, teacher handout, 
article, or research database. 
Study Guide 
A structured review document that highlights the most important concepts, vocabulary, 
and ideas students should remember. 
Summarization 
The process of shortening information while keeping the main points. AI tools are often 
used to summarize notes, readings, or complex topics. 
Technology Literacy 
The ability to understand and use technology effectively. This includes not just operating 
tools, but knowing how and when to use them wisely. 
Training 
The process of teaching an AI model using large amounts of data so it can recognize 
patterns and perform useful tasks. 
Training Data 
The information used during the training of an AI system. The quality and variety of 
training data can affect how the system performs. 
Tutor 
A person—or in some cases, a tool—that helps guide learning, explain ideas, and 
support skill development. AI can act like a tutor when used well.

Chapter 3 — AI as a Learning Partner 
 
Key Question 
How can students use artificial intelligence to support learning instead of simply getting 
answers? 
Learning Objectives 
 Understand the difference between using AI for answers and using AI for 
learning 
 Recognize how AI can support curiosity, practice, and understanding 
 Learn how follow-up questions improve learning 
 Begin thinking of AI as a study partner rather than a shortcut 
Introduction 
When students first begin using artificial intelligence, many treat it like a faster search 
engine or a homework shortcut. They type in a question, read the answer, and move on. 
That may feel efficient, but it often leads to shallow learning. The real value of AI is not 
that it can give answers quickly. The real value is that it can help students think more 
deeply, ask better questions, and explore ideas in ways that are more interactive than a 
textbook or worksheet alone. 
A good learning partner does not simply hand over answers. A good learning partner 
helps clarify ideas, supports practice, asks useful questions, and makes it easier to 
understand where confusion begins. AI can play that role well—if students use it 
intentionally.

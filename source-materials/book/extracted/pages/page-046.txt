Why This Chapter Matters 
Writing is one of the clearest places where AI can either strengthen communication or 
weaken independence. 
If students use AI to brainstorm, organize, revise, and clarify their writing, they can 
become stronger communicators. But if they rely on AI to do too much of the drafting 
or decision-making, they risk losing the chance to develop the very writing skills school 
is meant to strengthen. 
This chapter matters because it teaches students how to use AI for support without 
giving away ownership of their ideas. 
Chapter Summary 
In this chapter, you learned how AI can support brainstorming, outlining, revision, and 
clearer communication. You also explored why student voice matters and why AI 
should be used to strengthen writing rather than replace it. 
In the next chapter, we will shift from writing to another major academic skill: solving

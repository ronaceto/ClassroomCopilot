Why This Chapter Matters 
Coding is one of the clearest examples of how AI can either strengthen learning or 
weaken it. 
If students use AI only to generate code quickly, they may become more dependent and 
less confident. But if they use AI to understand code, debug errors, and practice small 
examples, they can become much stronger programmers over time. 
That is why this chapter matters. It shows students how to use AI in a way that builds 
skill instead of bypassing it. 
Chapter Summary 
In this chapter, you learned how AI can help students understand code, debug errors, 
and build small programming projects step by step. You also explored why copying code 
without understanding it weakens learning and why AI works best as a coding coach 
rather than a coding replacement. 
In the next chapter, we will look at how AI can support creativity and brainstorming by 
helping students generate ideas, explore possibilities, and develop projects in more 
imaginative ways.

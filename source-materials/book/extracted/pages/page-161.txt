Plagiarism 
Presenting someone else’s words, ideas, or work as your own without proper credit. AI 
use can create plagiarism risks if students submit work they did not truly create or 
understand. 
Prediction 
The process by which an AI system estimates what word, idea, or result is most likely to 
come next based on learned patterns. 
Productivity 
The ability to complete tasks effectively and efficiently. AI can support productivity by 
helping students plan, organize, and manage work. 
Programming 
Writing instructions that tell a computer what to do. Programming is often done using 
coding languages such as Python or JavaScript. 
Prompt 
The question, instruction, or request given to an AI system. The quality of the prompt 
often affects the quality of the response. 
Prompt Engineering 
The skill of writing and improving prompts so that AI tools produce clearer, more 
accurate, or more useful results. 
Research 
The process of gathering, organizing, and evaluating information in order to answer a 
question or better understand a topic. 
Responsible AI Use 
Using AI in ways that are honest, thoughtful, and supportive of real learning. 
Responsible use includes verifying information, protecting privacy, and maintaining 
academic integrity.

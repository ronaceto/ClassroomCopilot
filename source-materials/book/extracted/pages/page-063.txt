Why Brainstorming Matters 
Many students struggle most at the beginning of a project. 
They may be assigned a topic but have no idea what angle to take. They may need to 
write a story, build a presentation, design an experiment, or create a project, but feel 
stuck because they cannot think of a strong starting point. 
This is where brainstorming matters. 
Brainstorming is the process of generating many possible ideas before choosing the 
strongest one. The purpose is not to find the perfect answer immediately, but to create 
options that students can compare, combine, or reshape into something more original. 
AI can be very effective during this stage because it can produce multiple suggestions 
quickly. A student might ask: 
Give me five creative science project ideas related to renewable energy. 
Or: 
Help me brainstorm story ideas about a future where humans and robots work together. 
In both cases, AI is not doing the final creative work. It is helping the student move 
from zero ideas to several possible directions. 
That shift is often enough to unlock momentum. 
Using AI to Expand an Idea 
AI becomes even more useful after a student already has a starting point. 
A student may have one rough idea but not know how to develop it further. In that 
situation, AI can help expand the idea by suggesting details, variations, or next steps. 
For example, a student might ask: 
Turn this science fair idea into three different project directions. 
Or: 
Expand this short story idea into a beginning, middle, and end. 
These kinds of prompts allow students to test possibilities without committing too early. 
They can compare options, borrow one strong element, reject the weaker ones, and 
move forward with a clearer vision.

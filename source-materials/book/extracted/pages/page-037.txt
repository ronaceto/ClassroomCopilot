A student might begin by asking for a short explanation of mitosis. Then they might ask 
for five quiz questions. After missing two of them, they could ask for a simpler

whether they improved. 
That process is much stronger than reading the same notes over and over.

Classroom Idea 
Teachers can use this chapter to help students see how AI becomes most useful when it 
supports practice rather than just explanation. For example, after asking for a simple 
explanation of the water cycle, students could ask AI to create five quiz questions and 
then explain any missed answers. That turns AI from an answer source into a learning 
system built around explanation, practice, feedback, and improvement. 
Another effective use of AI is asking it to organize information into study-friendly 
forms. For example, students can ask AI to summarize a chapter, turn notes into 
flashcards, create a short review sheet, list the most important terms to remember, or

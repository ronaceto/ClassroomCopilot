Student Example 
A student named Sophia is preparing for a test on the causes of World War I and also 
needs to write a short paragraph response for class. 
She uses several prompts from this chapter: 
First, she asks: 
Give me a clear overview of the main causes of World War I. 
Then she asks: 
Create a five-question practice quiz on this topic. 
Later, when writing her paragraph, she asks: 
Suggest one way I can make this paragraph more clear without changing my voice. 
In this example, Sophia is not using AI for just one task. She is using it as a flexible 
toolkit for understanding, studying, and improving her writing. 
That is exactly what this chapter is designed to support.

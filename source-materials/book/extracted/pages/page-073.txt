Classroom Idea 
Teachers can use AI productively by having students compare an AI-generated work 
plan with one they create themselves. 
For example, students might be given a sample project and asked to create their own 
step-by-step schedule first. Then they ask AI to generate a second version. The class can 
compare: 
 Which plan is more realistic? 
 Which plan is more detailed? 
 What did the AI do well? 
 What would a student still need to adjust? 
This helps students see AI as a planning tool while still practicing project management 
skills of their own. 
Try This 
Prompt Example 
Help me break a large school assignment into smaller steps I can complete over one 
week. 
 
Then ask: 
Prompt Example 
Now turn those steps into a simple schedule I could follow. 
 
As you review the response, ask yourself: 
 Does this plan feel realistic? 
 Which steps would I change? 
 What would make this plan more useful for me personally? 
This helps you use AI not just to make a plan, but to think critically about what a 
good plan looks like.

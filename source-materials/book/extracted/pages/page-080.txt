Try This 
Try using the prompt below as a starting point, then compare the response to the text 
itself and to your own interpretation before deciding which ideas are actually useful. 
Prompt Example 
I’m writing a literary analysis paragraph about a symbol in a novel. Help me 
brainstorm two possible interpretations, then suggest one question I should answer 
to make my paragraph stronger. 
After reading the response, ask yourself: 
 Which ideas seem useful? 
 Which ideas actually match the text? 
 What would I still need to explain in my own words? 
Chapter Summary 
In this chapter, you learned that AI can support English language arts by helping 
students understand texts, brainstorm ideas, revise writing, and prepare for discussion. 
You also learned that student voice and interpretation still matter deeply in ELA. AI 
works best as a reading and writing coach, not as a replacement for reading, thinking, or 
composing.

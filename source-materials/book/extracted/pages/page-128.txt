15. Simulate a historical interview 
Act as [historical figure] during [time period] and answer my questions as if I were 
interviewing you. 
Why it works: 
This makes history more interactive and encourages perspective-based questioning. 
Prompts for Writing and Revision 
These prompts are useful when students are brainstorming, outlining, revising, 
or strengthening their writing. 
16. Brainstorm ideas 
Help me brainstorm three possible ideas for an essay or project on this topic. 
Why it works: 
This helps students get unstuck at the beginning of the writing process. 
17. Create an outline 
Create a simple outline for a high school essay on this topic. 
Why it works: 
This provides structure without replacing student thinking. 
18. Improve clarity 
Suggest two ways I can improve the clarity of this paragraph without changing my voice. 
Why it works: 
This supports revision while keeping the student’s writing at the center. 
19. Strengthen an argument 
Tell me how I can make this argument stronger and more convincing. 
Why it works: 
This helps students improve reasoning and evidence rather than just polish wording.

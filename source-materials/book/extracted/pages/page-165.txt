About the Author 
Ronnie “Ron” Aceto is a technology leader, educator, and advocate for thoughtful, 
responsible artificial intelligence use in education. 
With more than 30 years of experience in software, digital systems, and leadership, Ron 
has spent much of his career helping organizations solve complex problems through 
technology. His background includes software development, product leadership, systems 
thinking, and guiding teams through innovation in real-world environments. 
After a long career in technology, Ron turned his focus toward education and student 
learning. He is especially interested in helping young people understand how artificial 
intelligence can be used not as a shortcut, but as a tool for learning, creativity, problem 
solving, and future readiness. 
Ron believes students need more than access to AI tools. They need guidance in how to 
ask better questions, think critically about the answers they receive, and use technology 
in ways that strengthen their own understanding and independence. That belief led to 
the creation of AI for Students: A Practical Guide for School and Life. 
Drawing on both his technology background and his work in education, Ron writes with 
a practical, student-centered approach. His goal is to help students, teachers, and schools 
navigate the rapidly changing world of AI with clarity, confidence, and responsibility. 
Ron lives in Tennessee with his family and continues to work at the intersection of 
technology, learning, and innovation.

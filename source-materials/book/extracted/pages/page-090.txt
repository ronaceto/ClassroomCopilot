Try This 
Use the prompt below as a way to begin historical inquiry, not to end it. After 
reading the response, pay attention to what feels informative, what seems incomplete, 
and what still needs source support. 
Prompt Example 
Give me a simple overview of this historical event, identify two different perspectives 
on it, and suggest one source or type of source I should check to verify the 
explanation. 
After reading the response, ask yourself: 
 What seems accurate and useful? 
 What still needs evidence or source support? 
 Whose perspective might be missing? 
Chapter Summary 
In this chapter, you learned that AI can support history and social studies by helping 
students build background knowledge, compare perspectives, organize timelines, and 
explore cause and effect. You also learned that this subject requires careful verification 
because historical understanding depends on evidence, context, and multiple viewpoints.

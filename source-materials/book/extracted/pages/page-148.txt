Claude 
Claude is another AI chat assistant that many students find useful for explanation, 
writing support, and thoughtful analysis. It often performs well when students want a 
clear, calm explanation of a complex topic or need help revising writing in a more 
natural tone. 
Students may use Claude to: 
 understand complex ideas in simpler language 
 revise paragraphs for clarity 
 compare ideas or perspectives 
 summarize long passages 
 brainstorm stronger arguments in writing 
Claude can be especially useful when students want responses that feel reflective and 
well organized. 
Gemini 
Gemini is an AI tool from Google that may be especially useful for students who already 
work heavily within Google tools and workflows. It can support writing, brainstorming, 
summarizing, and general question answering. 
Students may find Gemini helpful for: 
 reviewing topics 
 organizing ideas 
 exploring broad questions 
 generating quick summaries 
 preparing first drafts or outlines 
Because it connects conceptually to Google’s broader ecosystem, some students may 
encounter it through school or personal accounts more naturally than other tools.

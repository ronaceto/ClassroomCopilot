Why This Chapter Matters 
Creativity is one of the most valuable human strengths in the age of AI. 
That may sound surprising, but it makes sense. As tools become better at generating 
information quickly, the ability to imagine, choose, refine, and create meaningful work 
becomes even more important. 
This chapter matters because it shows students how to use AI to expand their creativity 
without handing creativity away. It teaches them to use AI for inspiration, not imitation. 
Chapter Summary 
In this chapter, you learned how AI can support brainstorming, idea development, 
project planning, and creative exploration across many subjects. You also explored why 
student choice and original thinking still matter, even when AI is helping generate 
possibilities. 
In the next chapter, we will look at how AI can help students stay organized, manage 
projects, and become more productive without losing focus or independence.

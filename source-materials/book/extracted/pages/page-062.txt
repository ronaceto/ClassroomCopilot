Chapter 10 — AI for Creativity and Brainstorming 
 
Key Question 
How can students use AI to generate ideas and explore creativity without letting the tool 
take over the creative process? 
Learning Objectives 
 Understand how AI can support brainstorming and idea generation 
 Learn how AI can help students explore multiple creative directions 
 Recognize the difference between inspiration and dependence 
 Use AI to expand imagination while keeping student choice at the center 
Introduction 
Creativity is often misunderstood. 
Some people think creativity is something you either have or do not have. Others 
imagine it only belongs in art class, creative writing, or music. In reality, creativity 
matters in almost every subject students study. It appears whenever students design a 
project, develop an original argument, solve a problem in a new way, or try to explain an 
idea more clearly. 
Creativity is not just about making something beautiful. It is about generating 
possibilities. 
This is one area where AI can be surprisingly helpful. 
Artificial intelligence can help students brainstorm project concepts, presentation 
directions, experiment variations, and creative possibilities when they are not yet ready 
to draft a final product. 
But creativity is also an area where students need to stay in control. 
If AI generates everything, the work may become polished but empty. The real value 
comes when AI helps students expand their thinking while the student still chooses, 
develops, and shapes the final result. This chapter explores how to use AI in exactly that 
way.

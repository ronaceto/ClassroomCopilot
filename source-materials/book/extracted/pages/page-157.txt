Glossary of AI Terms 
How to use this glossary 
Students do not need to memorize every definition in this section. 
Instead, this glossary is meant to be a reference tool. Readers can return to it 
whenever they encounter a word in the book that feels unfamiliar or when they 
want a quick reminder of a key concept. 
Teachers may also find it useful when introducing vocabulary or reviewing 
important ideas related to AI literacy. 
Algorithm 
A set of instructions a computer follows to solve a problem or complete a task. 
Algorithms are the step-by-step logic behind many digital tools, including AI systems. 
Artificial Intelligence (AI) 
Computer systems designed to perform tasks that usually require human intelligence, 
such as understanding language, recognizing patterns, generating ideas, or making 
predictions. 
AI Assistant 
A digital tool that uses artificial intelligence to help users complete tasks such as 
answering questions, explaining concepts, organizing information, or generating content. 
AI Literacy 
The ability to understand what AI is, how it works at a basic level, how to use it 
responsibly, and how to evaluate the quality of its output. 
Automation 
Using technology to complete tasks automatically with less direct human effort. AI often 
increases automation by helping computers handle more complex tasks. 
Bias 
A tendency toward unfairness, imbalance, or incomplete representation. In AI, bias can 
appear when a system reflects patterns in its training data that lead to unfair or 
inaccurate results.

Classroom Idea 
Teachers can turn this chapter into a practical workshop by having students work in 
small groups to test and compare prompts. 
For example, each group could be assigned one category: 
 learning 
 studying 
 research 
 writing 
 coding 
 creativity 
Students could try one or two prompts, examine the responses, and then discuss: 
 Which prompt worked best? 
 What made it effective? 
 How could it be improved? 
 Which prompts support learning most clearly? 
This helps students move from simply using AI to understanding how and why good 
prompts work. 
Try This 
Try three prompts from different categories in this chapter: one for understanding, one 
for studying, and one for writing or creativity. As you compare the responses, ask 
yourself which prompt gave you the most useful help, which felt too broad, and what 
you would change next time. This helps you begin building your own personal prompt 
toolkit.

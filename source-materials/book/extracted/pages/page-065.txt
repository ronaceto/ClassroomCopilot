Asking Better Creative Prompts 
Just like in other chapters, better prompts lead to better results. 
A weak creative prompt might be: 
Give me an idea for a project. 
That may produce something generic. 
A stronger prompt might be: 
Give me three creative project ideas about climate change for a high school 
presentation, and make each one different in style. 
That prompt gives the AI a clearer task, audience, and direction. 
Students can also improve creative prompts by asking for contrast. For example: 
 Give me one realistic idea, one unusual idea, and one funny idea. 
 Suggest three project ideas with different levels of difficulty. 
 Show me three different ways to approach this topic. 
These kinds of prompts often produce more interesting results because they ask the AI 
to vary its response.

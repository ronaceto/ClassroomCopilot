AI for Goal Setting and Planning 
One of the most practical uses of AI in wellness is planning. 
Students often need help turning broad goals into specific actions. AI can help organize 
a plan, create a checklist, or break a wellness goal into manageable steps. 
For example, a student might ask: 
Help me create a simple weekly plan for improving hydration and sleep habits. 
Or: 
Give me a checklist for getting ready for a fitness assessment at school. 
These prompts can support self-management and reflection, especially when students 
are learning how healthy routines connect to school, sports, and daily life. 
Using AI for Reflection 
Health and wellness learning often includes reflection. Students may think about habits, 
challenges, goals, or how they feel before and after making changes. 
AI can support reflection by helping students organize thoughts or generate questions 
for journaling. For example: 
Give me three reflection questions about how stress affects my focus at school. 
Or: 
Help me reflect on what makes it easier or harder for me to stay active each 
week. 
These are useful because they help students think more clearly about habits and choices. 
The goal is not to let AI decide what the student feels. The goal is to help the student 
reflect more thoughtfully.

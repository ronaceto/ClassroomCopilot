Student AI Agreement 
1. Use AI to learn, not replace thinking. 
2. Be honest about my work and follow teacher directions. 
3. Verify important information before trusting it. 
4. Ask better questions and use AI to deepen understanding. 
5. Protect personal information and respect the privacy of others. 
6. Use AI responsibly at school and at home. 
Teacher Note 
This curriculum is built around the idea that students need AI literacy, not AI shortcuts. 
It is designed to help students use AI as a learning coach, thinking partner, and feedback 
tool while preserving academic integrity. Because access, age permissions, privacy 
controls, and tool availability vary by district and platform, the language in this book 
assumes that students should always follow local school expectations first. 
Parent/Caregiver Note 
Students are likely to encounter AI tools in school, at home, and in future workplaces. 
This book teaches students to use AI with honesty, verification, judgment, and care. 
When tools such as ChatGPT or similar platforms are discussed for home use, students 
should follow family rules, age requirements, and parent or guardian guidance. The goal 
is not to help students avoid learning, but to help them learn more thoughtfully and 
effectively.

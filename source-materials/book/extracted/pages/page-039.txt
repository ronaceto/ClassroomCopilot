Classroom Idea 
Teachers can use AI as a study support tool by asking students to compare study 
methods. 
For example, one group could review a topic by rereading notes, while another group 
uses AI to generate questions and explain mistakes. Students can then reflect on which 
approach helped them remember more and why. 
Try This 
Prompt Example 
Create a 5-question practice quiz about photosynthesis for a high school biology 
student. 
Then ask AI to explain the answers and why each one is correct. Notice how this 
method forces you to think, remember, and correct mistakes. That is what strong 
studying looks like. 
Many students spend a lot of time studying without always using the most effective 
methods. AI can help change that by making studying more active, flexible, and 
responsive. 
When students use AI to quiz themselves, explain difficult concepts, and learn from 
mistakes, they become more engaged in the learning process. Instead of studying 
passively, they begin practicing in ways that build stronger memory and deeper 
understanding. 
That is why AI works best as a study coach, not a shortcut. 
Chapter Summary 
In this chapter, you learned how AI can help students study more effectively by 
supporting active recall, clarifying difficult concepts, generating practice questions, and 
creating useful feedback loops. 
The goal is not to let AI study for you. The goal is to use AI to make your studying 
more active, focused, and effective. 
In the next chapter, we will look at how AI can support students when solving complex 
problems in subjects like math and science.

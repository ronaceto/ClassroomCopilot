Why This Matters for Students 
Students do not need to become AI engineers to use AI effectively. But they do need a 
basic understanding of how these systems work so they can ask better questions, use AI 
as a learning tool, verify important information, recognize when a response may be 
flawed, and avoid overtrusting the technology. That is one of the central goals of this 
book.

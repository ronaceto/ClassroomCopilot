Why This Chapter Matters 
Students are growing up in a world where AI will likely be part of many careers, whether 
they go into medicine, education, business, technology, design, or skilled trades. 
That does not mean students need to fear the future. It means they should prepare for it. 
The students who thrive will not necessarily be the ones who know the most technical 
terms. They will be the ones who can think clearly, adapt quickly, use tools wisely, and 
continue learning over time. 
That is why this chapter matters. It helps students see AI not as a threat to their future, 
but as part of the environment they must learn to navigate with skill and confidence.

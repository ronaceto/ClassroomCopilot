Chapter Summary 
In this chapter, you learned that AI systems are trained on large amounts of data, use 
pattern recognition to analyze language, and generate responses through prediction 
rather than true understanding. You also learned why AI can sometimes produce 
confident but incorrect answers, and why students should verify important information 
instead of assuming every response is accurate. 
In the next chapter, we will build on this idea by exploring how AI can become a 
learning partner when students use it thoughtfully and ask questions that deepen 
understanding.

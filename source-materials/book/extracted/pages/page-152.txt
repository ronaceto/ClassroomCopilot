AI Writing Tools 
Some AI tools are designed specifically to help with writing, editing, and organization. 
These tools are often best used after a student has already started writing. They can 
help improve clarity, grammar, structure, and tone, but they should not replace the 
student’s own ideas and voice. 
Examples include: 
 Grammarly 
 Notion AI 
Grammarly 
Grammarly is useful for helping students improve grammar, sentence clarity, and writing 
flow. It is less about generating ideas from scratch and more about polishing work that 
already exists. 
Students can use Grammarly to: 
 correct grammar issues 
 improve sentence structure 
 strengthen word choice 
 reduce awkward phrasing 
 make writing more clear and readable 
It is especially useful during revision, but students should still decide which suggestions 
actually improve their writing. 
Notion AI 
Notion AI is useful for students who want help organizing information, notes, tasks, 
and writing ideas in one place. Because Notion is built around planning and structured 
organization, its AI features can support both productivity and writing. 
Students can use Notion AI to: 
 summarize notes 
 generate outlines 
 organize project ideas 
 build study systems 
 track tasks and deadlines 
It can be especially useful for students who like keeping schoolwork, project planning, 
and writing support in one system.

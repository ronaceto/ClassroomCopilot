Chapter 4 — The Power of Good Prompts 
 
Key Question 
How does the way you ask a question affect the quality of an AI response? 
Learning Objectives 
 Understand what a prompt is 
 Learn why clear prompts produce better results 
 Recognize the parts of an effective prompt 
 Practice improving weak prompts 
Introduction 
Artificial intelligence tools can be incredibly helpful, but they are only as useful as the 
instructions they receive. If you ask a vague question, you will usually get a vague 
answer. If you ask a thoughtful, specific question, the response is much more likely to be 
useful. Prompting is not just a technical skill. It is also a thinking skill, because writing a 
good prompt requires students to know what they want, what they need help with, and 
how to guide the tool in the right direction. 
What Is a Prompt? 
A prompt is the instruction, question, or request you give to an AI tool. Some prompts 
are short and simple, but stronger prompts give the AI more direction by defining the 
topic, audience, and format of the response. 
Compare these two prompts: “Explain climate change.” and “Explain climate change in 
simple terms for a high school student and include three examples of real-world effects.” 
The second version is better because it gives the AI a clearer job to do. 
Why Better Prompts Matter 
Good prompts help students get more accurate explanations, save time by reducing 
confusion, receive answers at the right difficulty level, and generate more useful 
examples. In school, that means stronger studying, clearer writing, and more targeted 
feedback.

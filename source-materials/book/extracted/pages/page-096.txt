Chapter 16 — AI in PE, Health, and Personal 
Wellness 
 
Key Question 
How can students use AI to support fitness, health learning, goal setting, and personal 
wellness in safe, thoughtful ways without replacing professional or teacher guidance? 
Learning Objectives 
 Understand how AI can support wellness reflection, planning, and health concept 
review. 
 Use AI to organize goals, build routines, and clarify health information in age-
appropriate ways. 
 Recognize the limits of AI in topics that require teacher, coach, counselor, or 
medical guidance. 
 Identify responsible uses of AI in physical education and wellness learning. 
 Protect privacy and avoid unsafe dependence when using AI for personal topics. 
Introduction 
Physical education, health, and personal wellness are different from many other school 
subjects because they connect directly to daily habits, decision-making, and well-being. 
Students are not just learning information. They are learning how movement, nutrition, 
sleep, stress, safety, and self-awareness affect their lives. 
AI can support learning in this area by helping students understand concepts, reflect on 
goals, organize routines, and think more clearly about healthy choices. But this subject 
also requires careful limits. Health and wellness are personal. Some questions belong 
with a teacher, coach, school counselor, nurse, or medical professional, not with an AI 
tool. 
That means students should use AI here mainly for education, reflection, planning, and 
general support—not for diagnosis, treatment, or deeply private decisions. 
Students often connect with this subject most when they can see how it affects everyday 
life. A lesson on hydration matters more during sports practice. A lesson on sleep 
matters more before exams. A lesson on stress matters more during a packed school 
week. AI can support those connections by helping students reflect, plan, and turn 
general health ideas into realistic routines.

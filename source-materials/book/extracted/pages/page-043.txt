Protecting Student Voice 
Student writing should still sound like the student. 
This is one of the most important rules in this chapter. 
A strong essay is not just correct. It reflects the student’s thinking, word choice, and 
interpretation. If AI rewrites too much, the work may begin to sound polished but 
generic. It may lose the personality, originality, or perspective that makes student writing 
meaningful. 
That is why students should use AI carefully when revising. It is fine to ask for 
suggestions, examples, or alternate ways to phrase something, but the final writing 
should still feel like the student’s own work. 
A useful way to think about it is this: 
AI can help you become a stronger writer. 
AI should not become your replacement writer. 
AI for Style, Audience, and Communication 
Beyond generating ideas, AI can also help students think about how writing should 
sound for a particular audience and purpose. 
Students can also use AI to: 
 adjust tone for a formal or informal audience 
 strengthen introductions and conclusions 
 improve transitions between ideas 
 clarify word choice and sentence flow 
 turn notes into clearer speaking points for presentations 
 revise writing so it better matches the assignment purpose 
For example, a student might ask: 
Help me make this introduction sound more formal for a school audience. 
Or: 
Suggest two ways to make my conclusion clearer and more persuasive without changing 
my main point. 
These kinds of prompts help students think more carefully about audience, structure, 
and communication. AI can be especially helpful when students are trying to make their 
writing clearer or more effective.

Student Example 
A student named Jada enjoys both art and technology but is unsure how those interests 
could connect in a future career. 
She asks AI: 
What careers combine creativity and technology? 
The AI suggests careers such as user experience design, digital marketing, animation, 
game design, and product design. Jada then follows up: 
What does a user experience designer actually do? 
And later: 
What classes and skills would help a high school student prepare for that kind of job? 
In this example, AI helps Jada move from a vague interest to more focused career 
exploration. It does not choose her path for her, but it gives her language, possibilities, 
and direction she can use in future conversations.

Recommended AI Tools for Students 
Artificial intelligence tools are becoming part of everyday learning. Students can now use 
AI to ask questions, study for tests, organize ideas, improve writing, generate images, 
practice coding, and explore possible careers. But not every AI tool does the same thing 
well. Some are strongest at conversation and explanation, while others are better for 
research, writing, design, or productivity. That is why students need more than a list of 
tools. They need a basic understanding of what each type of tool is good for and how to 
choose the right one for the task in front of them. 
This section introduces several useful AI tools and categories of tools that students may 
encounter in school and beyond. The goal is not to encourage students to use every tool. 
The goal is to help them understand the AI landscape and make thoughtful choices. As 
always, AI tools are most valuable when they support learning rather than replace it. 
Tool access, age requirements, privacy settings, pricing, and district approval can change 
over time. Categories of tools often matter more than brand names, and students should 
always follow current school, family, and platform rules.

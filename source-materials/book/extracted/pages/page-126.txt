4. Compare two ideas 
Compare these two concepts in a way that makes the difference easy to understand. 
Why it works: 
Comparison helps students sort out details that may otherwise seem too similar. 
5. Identify common misunderstandings 
What is one thing students often misunderstand about this topic? 
Why it works: 
This prompt helps students focus on the part of a concept that is most likely to cause 
confusion. 
Prompts for Studying and Review 
These prompts are especially useful before quizzes, tests, or class reviews. 
6. Generate a quiz 
Create a five-question practice quiz on this topic for a high school student. 
Why it works: 
This turns AI into a study partner instead of just an explanation tool. 
7. Quiz one question at a time 
Quiz me on this topic one question at a time and wait for my answer before giving 
feedback. 
Why it works: 
This creates a more active and realistic study experience. 
8. Explain mistakes 
Explain why this answer is wrong and how I should think about it instead. 
Why it works: 
Students often learn more from mistakes than from correct answers.
